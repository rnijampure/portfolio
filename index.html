Two crafts, one instinct

About the writer behind the interface

For fifteen years, I worked inside digital products — as a UX and frontend architect and product designer — understanding what customers need, where they hesitate, and what makes an experience work for them. I've worked across UX, UI, interaction design, visual design, information architecture, and frontend implementation, adapting the end product based on both user needs and business goals.

Over the last few years I've leaned into AI-augmented writing tools — Claude, Sudowrite — to work faster without writing thinner, using reader feedback and retention the same way I'd use product analytics: to find exactly where a scene, a paragraph, or a screen is losing people, and rewrite until it doesn't.

Alongside my technology and design career, I’ve maintained a parallel practice in writing: children’s fantasy, historical romance, Hinglish family drama, curriculum content, technical documentation, and brand copy.

Over time, writing, design, and technology have become different expressions of the same instinct: understand the audience, communicate clearly, and create something that works.
