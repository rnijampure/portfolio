A bakery in Koramangala wanted more customers and an easier way to take orders. I took that idea from first questions to a working beta.

The goal

Let people order fast, cheaper and in a user friendly (accessible) way.

Research, design, code and documentation: all by Rohini Nijampure.

Live beta

I asked 10 clients what gets in their way.

Three became personas, chosen to cover every age group, gender identity and socio-economic status. Four pain points kept coming up.

  • Healthier alternatives

    Still love sweets, want better options.

  • Offers and prices

    Coupons make exotic items reachable.

  • Updates on delivery

    Better bulk ordering and delivery updates.

  • Accessibility

    Elderly and less tech-savvy people struggle to order online.

Persona boards from my case study.

Then I made sense of it.

I followed Ritika, a working mother of two, as she orders for a party tonight. The journey map showed where today’s experience fails her.

  • Phone ordering is slow, and long explanations irritate her.
  • She worries the address and items were taken down correctly.
  • She keeps calling for delivery status and gets the same answer.

So the app needed

Fewer ordering stepsA quick list of past ordersOrder details by emailReal-time delivery updates

The beta: some ideas work, the rest are on the way.

Working today

  • Four Bangalore bakers and their products, with prices in ₹, ratings and vegetarian or vegan marks
  • Filters by category, baker and price, plus search
  • Product pages with a zoomable image, a message and special instructions
  • A cart with quantities and per-item notes
  • A responsive layout that works on phones

In the pipeline

  • Checkout and payment (the flow stops at the cart today)
  • Offers and crossed-out MRP pricing
  • Delivery updates and a PIN code check
  • Hindi option, text-size controls and order by phone
  • Quick reorder from past orders
  • Baker onboarding and stock checks at checkout
